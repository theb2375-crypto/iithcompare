1. Core Philosophy & Introduction

IIT Hyderabad (AI)

Offered by a dedicated Department of Artificial Intelligence founded in 2019[cite: 1]. It balances rigorous mathematical theory with software engineering algorithms, building a complete ecosystem for intelligent, autonomous, and data-driven computational systems[cite: 1].

IIT Delhi (ECM)

Offered by the Applied Mechanics department[cite: 1]. It focuses heavily on solving complex physical engineering problems using core mechanics laws combined with multi-scale mathematical modeling and computational execution[cite: 1].

2. Program Structure & Duration

IIT Hyderabad (AI)

A highly streamlined and specialized 4-year B.Tech path (configured for 2025 onwards) with a lightweight total requirement of 128 credits across 8 semesters to complete graduation requirements[cite: 1].

IIT Delhi (ECM)

A rigorous, foundational 4-year undergraduate program spanning 8 to 12 semesters[cite: 1]. It features a traditional structural workload with a significantly higher minimum credit requirement of 157 credits[cite: 1].

3. Curriculum & Key Course Baskets

  • Mathematical ML: Dedicated tracks in Matrix Theory, Discrete Math, Probability, and a dual-course sequence in Optimization[cite: 1].

  • Science Baskets: Flexible modular science baskets (e.g., Modern Physics, Metrics Spaces, Linear Programming)[cite: 1].

IIT Delhi (ECM)

  • Physical Mechanics: Heavy focus on mechanical physics, including Solid Mechanics, Fluid Mechanics, and Theory of Elasticity[cite: 1].
  • Scientific Base: Extensive standard science core including Electrodynamics, Engineering Chemistry, and Biology[cite: 1].
  • Computational Tooling: Traditional math sequences combined with Numerical Methods and basic programming foundations[cite: 1].

4. Areas of Specialization & Electives

IIT Hyderabad (AI)

Highly customizable path featuring a substantial 30 credits of specialized Departmental Electives split across custom AI baskets, accompanied by 12 Free Elective credits for multidirectional academic growth[cite: 1].

IIT Delhi (ECM)

More structured domain pathways requiring 12 Departmental Elective credits (focusing on tracks like Vibration & Control, Aircraft Structures, and Chaos) alongside 9 Open Credits to round out the engineering degree[cite: 1].

5. Hands-on, Labs & Projects

IIT Hyderabad (AI)

Practical software implementation is deeply integrated directly into high-level programming paths, featuring long-running applied project core tracks such as the dedicated AI Project sequence spanning advanced semesters[cite: 1].

IIT Delhi (ECM)

Emphasizes physical prototyping alongside simulation. Features mandatory practical setups like Fluids and Solids Lab, Advanced Experiments, and intensive programming labs embedded inside FEM and CFD course modules[cite: 1].

6. Emerging Technologies Integration

IIT Hyderabad (AI)

Directly integrates next-generation digital intelligence architectures as standard core topics, including Deep Learning, Reinforcement Learning, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, and Robotics[cite: 1].

IIT Delhi (ECM)

Bridges physical laws with computing through a dedicated emerging trends component course: AML405: Machine Learning in Mechanics, teaching students to apply modern data science models to structural and physical systems[cite: 1].
